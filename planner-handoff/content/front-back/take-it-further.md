# Take It Further with Homestead Keeper

You've planned your year on paper. Homestead Keeper keeps it alive.

Everything in this planner maps straight to the app, in the same words, so nothing feels new
when you open it. The difference is what happens after you write it down.

## What the app does that paper can't
- **It schedules your garden for you.** Set your frost dates once and every Crop Planting gets
  its seed starting, transplant, and harvest windows worked out and dropped into Upcoming Work.
- **It remembers so you don't have to.** Care, sprays, service, and reviews come back around as
  reminders instead of living in your head.
- **It keeps each thing's whole story.** Every animal, planting, and piece of equipment gets a
  Timeline, and an animal's Current Status updates itself from its latest event.
- **It hands you clean records.** Export an animal's full record, or a season's, as a tidy PDF
  for a buyer, your vet, or your own files (with Pro).
- **It counts your shelves and your bins.** Inventory and Food & Pantry warn you before you run
  low or something expires.

## Get the app
Scan the code or visit homesteadkeeper.com to start your Homestead. Plan it here, keep it there.
