# A Few Words We Use

The planner uses the same words as the Homestead Keeper app, so the two feel like one thing.
Here's what they mean in plain terms.

- **Homestead** is your whole place, everything you manage.
- **Place** is a named spot on your homestead, like the barn, the garden, or the root cellar.
- **Specific Spot** is a finer location within a Place, like a nest box wall or row three.
- **Growing Area** is a place you grow food: a bed, a greenhouse, an herb garden.
- **Crop Planting** is one crop you've put in, with its variety, dates, and notes.
- **Field Log** is a quick note that something happened, like fed, pruned, or inspected.
- **Record** is a saved log entry with the details (date, cost, notes).
- **Timeline** is the story of one thing, newest first.
- **Reminder** is a task that's due and comes back on a schedule.
- **Upcoming Work** is what's due soon across a part of your homestead.
- **Current Status** is the living state of something: Active, Sold, Retired.
- **Lifecycle events** are the moments that change an animal's status: Acquired, Born, Weaned,
  Moved, Sold, Processed, Retired.

The hubs, by the way, are just the parts of your homestead: Garden, Orchard, Animals, Bees,
Property, Equipment, Inventory, Food & Pantry, Off-grid, Weather & Seasons, Wildlife & Pests,
Forestry, and Emergency Prep.
