# How to Use This Planner

Welcome. This planner is built to help you plan your whole homestead for the year, from the
garden to the animals to the pantry, and to make it easy to keep going once the season gets busy.

You can use it two ways. On its own, it's a complete paper planner, and every page works with a
pencil and a few minutes. Alongside the Homestead Keeper app, it's the paper front end. You plan
here, then the app keeps the record alive, updates itself, and reminds you what's due.

## How it's organized
- **Start with the season.** The seasonal pages help you set your frost dates and see the whole
  year at a glance, so everything else lands at the right time for where you live.
- **Then work through the hubs.** There's a page for each part of your homestead, in the same
  names the app uses: Garden, Animals, Orchard, Food & Pantry, Property, Equipment, Inventory,
  Emergency Prep, and a few more.
- **Print the templates you need.** The reusable pages (like Individual Animal and Crop Planting)
  are meant to be printed as many times as you like, one per animal, one per planting, one per
  season.

## A few things worth knowing
- **Dates work from your frost date, not the calendar.** A homestead in the north and one in the
  south are on different clocks. Fill in your frost dates first and the timing follows.
- **The little "logged in app" box** on some pages is just a nudge to save it in Homestead Keeper
  when you get a chance, so nothing lives only on paper.
- **Nothing here requires the app.** The app makes it easier, but the planner stands on its own.

Grab a pencil and start wherever you like. Most folks start with the season and the garden.
