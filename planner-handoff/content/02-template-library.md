# Template Library

Ten reusable, print-on-demand pages. Each one maps to an app add-form and uses the app's own
words. Print as many as you need. Voice: human, no em-dashes (spec §0).

## 1. Individual Animal (print one per animal)

For a single animal you follow closely, like a milk goat, a breeding buck, or a guardian dog.
Fill in the top, then keep it in the barn.

Fields (same as adding an animal in the app): Name, Type (Individual, Flock, Herd, Hive,
Rabbitry, Guardian, Pet), Species and Breed, Sex, Date of Birth, Ear Tag or ID, Color and
Markings, Place, Specific Spot, Notes.

Timeline strip: dated lines for the events that matter, Acquired, Born, Weaned, Moved, Sold,
Processed, Retired.

Logs: a health and treatment log (date, what you did, cost) and a weigh-in log.

In the app: every entry lands on the animal's Timeline, its Current Status updates itself from
the latest event, and you can export the whole record as a clean PDF for a buyer or your vet.

## 2. Crop Planting (print one per planting, or per bed)

For anything you put in the ground and want to follow from seed to harvest.

Fields (same as adding a planting): Crop or Plant, Variety, Planted In (your Growing Area or
Place), Specific Spot, Source, Seed Start Date, Transplant Date, Harvest Window (opens and
closes), Notes.

A note up top: work your dates from your frost date, not the calendar.

Reminders to check: Watering check, Harvest check.

In the app: each planting gets its own Timeline, and the sow, transplant, and harvest windows
show up as Upcoming Work on their own.

## 3. Orchard Planting (print one per tree)

For fruit and nut trees you'll tend for years.

Fields (same as adding a planting in the Orchard): Name, Type (Fruit Tree, Nut Tree), Variety,
Rootstock, Place and Specific Spot, Plant Date, Notes.

Care log: dated lines for Prune, Spray, Harvest, Inspect, and Fertilize.

In the app: those same Field Log actions live under the tree, and your sprays and prunings
become Upcoming Work so you don't miss the window.

## 4. Breeding Record (print one per breeding)

For a planned or completed breeding, so you can see it through to birth.

Fields (same as a breeding record): Dam, Sire, Breeding Date, Method, Status (Planned,
Completed), Expected Due Date, Notes.

Gestation helper so you can do it by hand: chicken about 21 days, rabbit about 31 days, goat and
sheep about 150 days, pig about 114 days, cow about 283 days. Count from the breeding date.

In the app: the due date is worked out for you and rides along on the dam's Timeline as it gets
close.

## 5. Hive (print one per hive)

For each colony, with room to log inspections through the season.

Fields (same as adding a Hive): Hive name, Place and Specific Spot, ID or Tag, Install date.

Inspection log: dated lines for brood, stores, queen spotted, mite count, temperament, and any
action taken.

Reminders to check: Hive inspection, Feed check, Mite count.

## 6. Equipment and Asset (print one per tractor, tool, or system)

For the things you maintain and don't want to lose the history on.

Fields (same as adding Equipment): Name, Type, Place, ID or Tag, Brand or Make, Model, Serial
Number, Purchase Date, Purchase Cost, Where you buy it, Warranty expiration and provider, Notes.

Service log: dated lines for what you did, hours or mileage, parts, and cost.

Reminders to check: Oil and filter change, Annual service.

In the app: keep the service history in one place, attach receipts and the warranty PDF with
Pro, and let the reminders chase you.

## 7. Pantry and Preservation (print one per batch, or a sheet per shelf)

For what you can, freeze, dehydrate, and ferment, and what's in storage.

Fields (same as adding to Food and Pantry): Item, Type (Canned Goods, Frozen Food, Dehydrated
Food, Fermented Batch, Pantry Item, Root Cellar Item), Place or Storage note, Quantity and unit,
Date put up, Best by or expiration, Batch and source notes.

In the app: the Field Log actions (Can, Freeze, Dehydrate, Ferment, Store, Use) keep a running
count of the shelf, and expirations warn you before things turn.

## 8. Inventory Count Sheet (print one, refresh as you go)

A walk-around count of feed, seeds, medicine, and fuel. A simple grid, one row per item: Item,
Category (Feed, Bedding, Seeds, Soil and Amendments, Filters, Fuel, Animal Health), Quantity,
Unit, Low-stock at, Place, Notes.

In the app: Inventory flags what's low or expiring, so the count becomes a quick check instead
of a chore.

## 9. Emergency Prep Checklist (print one, review twice a year)

For water, food, fuel, first aid, plans, and contacts.

Fields per item (same as adding an Emergency item): Item, Type (Emergency Supply, Emergency
Food, Emergency Water, Fuel Reserve, Generator Plan, Evacuation Note, First Aid Supply), Location
or Specific Spot, Quantity, Expiration or review date, Notes.

Add a contacts block: family, neighbors, vet, power and water companies.

Reminders to check: Supply inventory check, Rotate and replenish.

## 10. Grow Season 20__ Overview (print one per season)

The one-page plan for the whole growing season. Up top, the season and year, your frost dates,
and two or three goals. Then an index of this season's plantings, one row each: Crop, Variety,
Growing Area, Sow date, Transplant date, Harvest window. Room at the bottom for what worked,
what didn't, and what to change next year.

In the app: every row here can become a Crop Planting, and the season fills in your Garden
Timeline and Upcoming Work.
