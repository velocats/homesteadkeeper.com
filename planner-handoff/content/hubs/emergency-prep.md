# Emergency Prep
*Water, food, fuel, first aid, plans, and contacts.*

You hope you never need it, which is exactly why it's easy to let slide. A twice a year review
keeps your kit honest.

## Plan it
- Decide how many days of water, food, and fuel you want on hand.
- Write down your plan (shelter in place, or where you'd go).
- Keep a current contact list and test your backup power.

## Prep checklist and contacts (fill in)
One row per item: Item, Type (Emergency Supply, Emergency Food, Emergency Water, Fuel Reserve,
Generator Plan, Evacuation Note, First Aid Supply), Location, Quantity, Expiration or review
date, Notes. Add a contacts block for family, neighbors, vet, and your power and water companies.
This is the Emergency Prep Checklist template.

## Through the season
Refresh water, food, and fuel in fall before storm season, and test the Generator Plan while the
weather is still fair.

## Keep it going in Homestead Keeper
Set review dates and they come back around as Upcoming Work, so your kit never quietly expires.
