# Inventory
*Feed, seeds, medicine, fuel, and supplies on hand.*

Inventory is the boring hub that saves your weekend, because running out of feed or a filter
always seems to happen at the worst time.

## Plan it
- Decide what you always want on hand, and your low-stock level for each.
- Set a restock cadence (feed before winter, seeds before spring).
- Note your vendors and rough costs.

## Count sheet (fill in)
One row per item: Item, Category (Feed, Bedding, Seeds, Soil and Amendments, Filters, Fuel,
Animal Health), Quantity, Unit, Low-stock at, Place, Notes. This is the Inventory Count Sheet
template.

## Through the season
Stock feed and bedding before winter, seeds and amendments before spring, and check your animal
health supplies before kidding and lambing.

## Keep it going in Homestead Keeper
Inventory flags what's low or expiring under Needs Attention, so a walk-around count becomes a
quick glance instead of a chore.
