# Wildlife & Pests (reference)
*Sightings, predator pressure, and habitat notes.*

Keeping a simple record of what you're seeing helps you spot a pattern before it becomes a
problem.

## What to jot down
- Pest Sightings (aphids, squash bugs, and the like) and where you saw them.
- Predator Sightings and any losses.
- Deer, rodent, and bird pressure through the season.
- Habitat notes (hedgerows, pollinators, corridors).

## Keep it going in Homestead Keeper
Log each one as a record under Wildlife & Pests so you can look back over the season and see the
trend.

There isn't a dedicated paper template for this hub. A blank note page or the app works fine.
