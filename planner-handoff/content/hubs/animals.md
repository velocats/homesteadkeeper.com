# Animals
*Care for animals and track their whole story.*

Your animals need the same care whether you write it down or not, so a little planning up front
saves a lot of scrambling later.

## Plan it
- List every animal or group you keep, with species, breed, and ID.
- Lay out your breeding plan and rough due dates for the year.
- Sketch a feed and health calendar (vaccinations, hoof trims, parasite checks).
- Note who you might sell or cull, and when.

## Animal Roster (fill in)
One row per animal or group: Name, Type, Species and Breed, Sex, Ear Tag or ID, Place, Notes.
Print the Individual Animal template for the ones you follow closely, and a Breeding Record for
each breeding.

## Through the season
Births come in spring, pasture and production peak through summer, weaning and sell decisions
land in late summer, and winter is for cold-weather care and watching due dates for spring.

## Keep it going in Homestead Keeper
Every animal gets a Timeline built from the events that matter (Acquired, Born, Weaned, Moved,
Sold, Retired), and its Current Status updates itself from the latest one. The app works out
breeding due dates for you and can export an animal's full record as a clean PDF for a buyer or
your vet.

Templates for this hub: Individual Animal, Breeding Record.
