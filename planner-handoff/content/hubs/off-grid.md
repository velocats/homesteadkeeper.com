# Off-grid (reference)
*Solar, water, power, and backup systems.*

Off-grid systems are quiet until they aren't. A short maintenance rhythm keeps the lights on and
the water running.

## Maintenance rhythm
- List your systems (solar array, batteries, well pump, generator, rain catchment).
- Monthly: a quick check of each system and its readings.
- Seasonally: service the generator, check battery levels, clean the panels, winterize the water.

## Keep it going in Homestead Keeper
Track each system like any other asset, keep its service history, and let the monthly and annual
checks turn into reminders.
