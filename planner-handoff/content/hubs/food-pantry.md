# Food & Pantry
*What you've canned, frozen, dehydrated, and stored.*

The Food & Pantry is where a good garden year turns into a well-stocked winter, if you keep
track of what you put up.

## Plan it
- Set rough goals for the year (how many jars, pounds, or meals).
- Match what you'll preserve to when it comes in.
- Note your storage spots (pantry, freezer, root cellar) and what goes where.

## Pantry inventory (fill in)
One row per item: Item, Type (Canned Goods, Frozen Food, Dehydrated Food, Fermented Batch,
Pantry Item, Root Cellar Item), Storage spot, Quantity and unit, Date put up, Best by, Notes.
Print the Pantry and Preservation template for batches you want to track.

## Through the season
Preserve hard through summer and late summer as the harvest rolls in, then draw down the shelves
through winter and spring.

## Keep it going in Homestead Keeper
The Field Log actions (Can, Freeze, Dehydrate, Ferment, Store, Use) keep a running count of
what's on the shelf, and best by dates warn you before anything turns.
