# Garden
*Plan, plant, and grow food in your Growing Areas.*

The Garden is where the year starts and where it keeps you busiest. Plan it once now and you'll
spend the season following the plan instead of guessing.

## Plan it
- List your Growing Areas (beds, greenhouse, herb garden) and draw a rough map.
- Decide what to grow and roughly how much of each.
- Check your Seeds in Inventory and note what to order.
- Pencil in succession crops so beds don't sit empty.
- Set your dates from your frost date, not the calendar.

## This season's plantings (fill in)
One row per planting: Crop or Plant, Variety, Growing Area, Sow date, Transplant date, Harvest
window, Notes. Print the Crop Planting template for anything you want to follow closely.

## Through the season
Start slow crops indoors in early spring, transplant and direct sow around your last frost, sow
in succession and harvest through summer, then put the Growing Areas to bed before your first
frost.

## Keep it going in Homestead Keeper
Each planting becomes a Crop Planting with its own Timeline, and once your frost dates are set
the app works out the seed starting, transplant, and harvest windows and drops them into
Upcoming Work. On paper you plan the year once. In the app it schedules itself.
