# Orchard
*Fruit and nut trees, from planting to harvest.*

Trees reward patience and punish neglect, so the Orchard is mostly about keeping a steady
schedule year after year.

## Plan it
- List your trees, where each one stands, and roughly when it bears.
- Set your spray and pruning schedule for the year.
- Plan any new plantings and where they'll go.

## Tree index (fill in)
One row per tree: Name, Type (Fruit Tree, Nut Tree), Variety, Rootstock, Place, Planted, Notes.
Print the Orchard Planting template for each tree.

## Through the season
Finish dormant pruning and a dormant oil spray in late winter, spray at petal fall, tend and
thin through summer, then harvest and clean up drops in late summer and fall.

## Keep it going in Homestead Keeper
The Field Log actions (Prune, Spray, Harvest, Inspect, Fertilize) live right under each tree, and
your sprays and prunings turn into Upcoming Work so you catch the narrow windows.
